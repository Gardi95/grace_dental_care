Frequently Asked Questions

Does the dental implant surgery procedure hurt? ▼

Not at all! Every implant procedure is conducted under localized anesthesia to ensure absolute pain-free surgery. Post-surgical discomfort is minimal and easily managed with standard painkillers within 2 to 3 days.

How long do clinical teeth whitening results last? ▼

Typically, clinical laser whitening results last between 1 to 2 years, depending largely on individual oral hygiene and dietary habits (e.g. coffee, tea, smoking). Regular scale removals and cleanings help maintain the white shine.

Are transparent clear aligners as effective as metal braces? ▼

Yes! Modern transparent clear aligners are highly engineered utilizing 3D computational tooth movement models. They are extremely effective for mild to moderate spacing or overcrowding issues while remaining practically invisible.

What sterilization clinical protocols do you enforce? ▼

Patient health is our absolute priority. We employ strict Class-B Autoclaves for high-temperature surgical sterilization on all metal instruments, continuous air filtration, and utilize medical-grade disposables for every separate patient.
