Our Story

Welcome to Grace Dental Care

Crafting Beautiful, Healthy Smiles Since 2011

Grace Dental Care was founded with a singular clinical vision: to make elite, modern dental care highly accessible and completely pain-free. We believe that visiting the dentist shouldn't evoke anxiety. By combining advanced clinical equipment with a warm, caring environment, we have successfully treated over 15,000 patients.

Our board-certified medical team covers all major fields, including orthodontic aligners, biocompatible dental implants, aesthetic veneers, and micro-endodontics. We strictly adhere to international sterilization controls (Class-B Autoclaves) to ensure safety and clinical longevity.
